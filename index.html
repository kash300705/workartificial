Questions, answered.

What does Work Artificial build, exactly?+

AIOS: an AI receptionist, appointment reminders, review automation, lead follow-up, a reporting dashboard, and WhatsApp/Instagram messaging, scoped to how your salon actually books and runs.

How fast do we go live?+

Days, not months. You bring your booking flow and common call types; we build against them, test on real cases, and go live before you commit long-term.

Do I need staff who understand tech?+

No. We build, run, and maintain the system. Your team keeps working the way they already do, the dashboard just tells them what happened.

What does it cost compared to hiring?+

A fraction of a front-desk hire's monthly cost, and it doesn't need training, sick days, or holiday cover. Pure monthly retainer, no setup fee.

What if it makes a mistake?+

Complex or sensitive calls get flagged to you directly. You see every action the system takes, nothing irreversible happens without your visibility.

How do you handle client data?+

Built around Swiss FADP requirements: a recording-disclosure line, a data processing agreement, and clear data-handling terms from day one.
