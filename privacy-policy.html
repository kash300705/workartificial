Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 24 August 2026

Work Artificial ("Work Artificial", "we", "us" or "our") respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website, contact us, request information about our services, or interact with systems operated by us on behalf of our clients.

This Privacy Policy explains the manner in which we collect, use, disclose and protect personal information. It applies to information collected through our website, enquiry and demonstration forms, direct communications with us, and, where applicable, through the AI-powered systems and business infrastructure that we provide to our clients.

Where applicable, our processing of personal information is carried out in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection ("FADP") and the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"). The FADP requires organisations to provide individuals with clear and accessible information concerning the collection and use of their personal information, including the purposes of processing and relevant recipients.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends upon the nature of your interaction with Work Artificial.

When you contact us, request a demonstration, submit an enquiry or otherwise communicate with us, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, business name, professional position, business address and any information contained within your correspondence or enquiry.

Where you become a client, we may additionally process information required to establish and administer our relationship with you, including account information, business requirements, operational information, appointment information, communications and other information necessary to configure and provide our services.

Our website may also collect certain technical information automatically when you visit it. This may include your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, approximate location, pages visited, referring website, time spent on the website and information concerning your interaction with its features. We may use cookies and similar technologies for this purpose.

Where Work Artificial provides an AI Operations System ("AIOS") to a client, the system may process information belonging to that client's customers. Depending upon the configuration of the system, this may include names, telephone numbers, email addresses, appointment details, enquiries, service requests, communication records and other information voluntarily provided during an interaction.

2. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information where necessary to respond to enquiries, arrange demonstrations, establish and administer client relationships, provide our services and communicate with clients and their customers.

Information processed through an AIOS may be used to facilitate customer enquiries, appointment scheduling, appointment reminders, follow-ups, customer service, review requests and other operational functions that have been configured for the relevant business.

We may also use information to maintain the security and reliability of our systems, investigate suspected misuse, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain appropriate business records.

We do not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected.

Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis for processing will depend upon the circumstances. This may include performance of a contract, taking steps at the request of an individual before entering into a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, our legitimate interests, or consent where consent is required. The GDPR requires organisations to identify and communicate the applicable legal basis for processing personal information.

3. Artificial Intelligence and Automated Systems

AI is central to the services provided by Work Artificial. Our systems may communicate with customers, interpret enquiries, organise information and perform operational tasks on behalf of a business.

Depending upon the configuration selected by the client, an AIOS may answer telephone calls, respond to enquiries, schedule or modify appointments, provide information supplied by the business, follow up with prospective customers, request reviews, issue reminders and assist with customer service.

The AI system operates according to the instructions, business information and rules provided to the relevant client. Work Artificial does not represent that an automated system will correctly interpret every enquiry or circumstance, and clients remain responsible for ensuring that the information supplied to their system is accurate and appropriate.

Where applicable law requires disclosure of automated processing or provides individuals with rights concerning automated decisions, we will provide the information and safeguards required by that law. Swiss data protection guidance specifically recognises transparency requirements in relation to AI-supported processing and certain automated individual decisions.

Work Artificial does not intentionally use customer information processed through a client's AIOS to train publicly available general-purpose AI models unless this is separately disclosed and permitted under the applicable contractual and legal framework.

4. Telephone and Voice Communications

Where our services include an AI voice receptionist or other telephone functionality, information communicated during a telephone interaction may be processed by Work Artificial and by the technology providers required to operate the service.

Depending upon the configuration of the relevant system, calls may be transcribed, recorded or otherwise processed for purposes including appointment management, customer service, quality assurance, troubleshooting, record keeping and the provision of the requested service.

Where recording or transcription requires notice or consent under applicable law, the relevant notice or consent mechanism will be implemented by the responsible business.

5. SMS and Electronic Communications

Where SMS functionality is provided, messages may be sent in connection with appointments, customer support, reminders, confirmations, cancellations, rescheduling and other services requested by the recipient.

Recipients will not receive promotional SMS communications where the necessary consent or other lawful basis has not been obtained.

A recipient may withdraw from SMS communications by replying STOP to the number from which the message was received. Assistance may be requested by replying HELP or by contacting Work Artificial at hello@workartificial.com.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency depends upon the recipient's interaction with the relevant business and the services being provided.

Work Artificial does not sell, rent or provide SMS opt-in information to third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Telephone numbers and consent records collected specifically in connection with SMS messaging may be provided to telecommunications and messaging providers where necessary to deliver the requested communications.

6. Service Providers and Disclosure of Information

Work Artificial uses selected technology providers to operate and deliver its services. Depending upon the services being provided, these providers may include providers of cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence, voice communications, SMS delivery, appointment scheduling, calendar systems, analytics, customer relationship management, payment processing and website infrastructure.

Such providers receive only the information reasonably necessary for the service they provide and are subject to appropriate contractual, technical or organisational safeguards where required.

We may also disclose personal information where required by law, legal process or a lawful request from a competent authority, or where disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property or security of Work Artificial, our clients or others.

In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring or sale of all or part of our business, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable confidentiality and data protection requirements.

When Work Artificial provides an AIOS to a business, the business generally determines what customer information is collected, the purposes for which it is used and the manner in which its customer relationships are managed.

In these circumstances, Work Artificial may process personal information on behalf of that business as a service provider or processor.

Accordingly, a person interacting with an AI receptionist, appointment system or other automated service operated for one of our clients may also be subject to that client's own privacy policy. The relevant client remains responsible for determining the appropriate notices, consents and lawful basis applicable to its particular use of customer information.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, Work Artificial will take the measures required by applicable data protection law to ensure that the transfer is subject to an appropriate level of protection and, where necessary, appropriate contractual or other safeguards.
